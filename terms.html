Cancellation & Refunds

Our cancellation policy is designed to be fair to both users and venue partners:

  • More than 12 hours before: Full refund to original payment method within 3-5 business days
  • 6–12 hours before: 75% refund; 25% cancellation fee retained
  • 2–6 hours before: 50% refund; 50% fee retained
  • Less than 2 hours before: No refund (slot cannot be reassigned)
  • Venue cancellation: 110% refund (including compensation) within 24 hours

Refunds are processed to the original payment instrument. UPI refunds typically take 1-2 business days; card refunds may take 5-7 business days.
