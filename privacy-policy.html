Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SportifyTurf Pvt. Ltd. ("SportifyTurf", "we", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you use our website and booking platform.
Contents
1. Information We Collect 2. How We Use Your Information 3. Information Sharing 4. Data Security 5. Cookies & Tracking 6. Your Rights 7. Data Retention 8. Children's Privacy 9. Changes to Policy 10. Contact Us
Section 01

Information We Collect

When you create an account, make a booking, or interact with our platform, we collect information in the following categories:

Personal Information

  • Full name, email address, and mobile phone number
  • Date of birth (optional, for age-restricted facilities)
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Billing address and payment information

Usage Data

  • Booking history, preferences, and search queries
  • Device information, IP address, and browser type
  • Pages visited, clicks, and time spent on our platform
  • Location data (with your permission) for nearby venue suggestions
Section 02

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Booking Processing: To confirm, manage, and facilitate your sports ground bookings
  • Account Management: To create and manage your account, and verify your identity
  • Communication: To send booking confirmations, reminders, and important updates
  • Platform Improvement: To analyze usage patterns and improve our services
  • Marketing: To send promotional offers (only with your consent)
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws and regulations
We will never sell your personal data to third parties for their marketing purposes.
Section 03

Information Sharing

We share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Venue Partners: Basic booking details (name, phone) are shared with the venue operator for your reservation
  • Payment Processors: Payment information is processed securely through Razorpay and never stored on our servers
  • Service Providers: Trusted third parties who help operate our platform (email services, analytics, hosting)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government authority
Section 04

Data Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously and implement industry-standard measures:

  • 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmission
  • PCI-DSS compliant payment processing through certified processors
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Two-factor authentication available for all accounts
  • Secure server infrastructure with access controls

Despite our efforts, no internet transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but will notify you promptly in case of any data breach affecting your information.

Section 05

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the platform to function (session management, security)
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with our platform (Google Analytics)
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings like theme and language preferences
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to show relevant advertisements (optional, opt-out available)

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings or our cookie preference center available at the bottom of our website.

Section 06

Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time
  • Restriction: Request that we restrict processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@sportifyturf.in or through your account settings.

Section 07

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for the following periods:

  • Active Account Data: Retained as long as your account is active
  • Booking Records: Retained for 7 years for accounting and tax compliance
  • Marketing Data: Retained until you withdraw consent
  • Deleted Accounts: Personal data removed within 30 days, aggregated analytics retained indefinitely
Section 08

Children's Privacy

SportifyTurf is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete such information.

Users between 13-18 years must obtain parental consent before creating an account or making bookings.

Section 09

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. We will:

  • Post the updated policy on this page with a new "Last Updated" date
  • Send you an email notification for material changes
  • Display a prominent notice on our platform for significant changes

Your continued use of SportifyTurf after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
