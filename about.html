Our Story

Born From a Missed Game

It started with a frustrating Saturday morning — our founder, Raj Kumar, called 12 different turf owners just to find an available football pitch. By the time he found one, half his team had gone home.

That day, SportifyTurf was born. A platform where every player, team, and sports lover could instantly find, book, and enjoy premium sports grounds — without the chaos. Today, we serve 15,000+ players across Chennai and are expanding across Tamil Nadu.

Our Journey

Key Milestones

2020

Founded

SportifyTurf launched with 5 venues in Anna Nagar, Chennai. First 100 bookings in a week.

2021

Series A Funding

Raised ₹5 Crore in seed funding. Expanded to 25 venues across 6 zones of Chennai.

2022

Cricket & Badminton Added

Platform expanded beyond football. Onboarded cricket grounds and badminton courts city-wide.

2024

10,000 Players Milestone

Crossed 10,000 registered players and 40,000 completed bookings. Launched team booking feature.

2026

120+ Venues & Expanding

Now serving 15,000+ active players across 120+ premium venues. Tamil Nadu expansion in progress.
